# 🌍 Global Airbnb Marketplace Review Dashboard

> **Power BI Business Intelligence project analyzing Airbnb's global marketplace across listings, pricing, cities, room types, hosts, reviews, and customer experience.**

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

The objective of this project is to transform Airbnb marketplace data into an **interactive business intelligence dashboard** that provides insights into marketplace growth, city performance, pricing, accommodation mix, and customer satisfaction.

The analysis answers key business questions such as:

* Which cities have the strongest marketplace presence?
* How does pricing vary across room types?
* Which accommodation types dominate the marketplace?
* Which cities generate the highest review activity?
* What are the key customer experience strengths and gaps?
* How has Airbnb's marketplace evolved over time?

## 📊 Data Source

**Maven Analytics – Airbnb Listings & Reviews**

The dataset contains Airbnb marketplace data across **10 major global cities**, including listings, hosts, room types, property types, pricing, reviews, ratings, and locations.

**Original data source:** Inside Airbnb

## 🔍 Dashboard Highlights

### 🏠 Marketplace Overview

* **279K+ Listings**
* **182K+ Hosts**
* **10 Cities**
* **144 Property Types**
* Entire Places account for approximately **65% of listings**
* Hotel rooms command the highest average rental price at approximately **$800**

### 📈 Listing & Marketplace Trends

* Tracks Airbnb listing growth and decline over time
* Highlights marketplace **Introduction → Growth → Maturation → Decline → Fall**
* Shows the impact of regulatory changes and global travel disruption on listing activity
* Analyzes host verification and multiple-listing activity

### ⭐ Ratings & Customer Experience

* Overall rating: **9.58**
* Check-in: **9.70**
* Communication: **9.70**
* Cleanliness: **9.31**
* Location: **9.63**
* **Mexico City** records the highest overall rating at **9.72**
* Cleanliness emerges as the key customer-experience improvement area

### 🌎 Most Reviewed Cities

Top reviewed markets include:

**Paris → Rome → New York → Mexico City → Sydney**

Paris leads the analyzed markets with **351K+ reviews**, highlighting strong historical guest engagement.

---

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways

* Airbnb's marketplace is strongly concentrated in major global destinations.
* **Entire Places and Private Rooms dominate marketplace supply.**
* Accommodation type has a significant impact on average rental pricing.
* Customer satisfaction is generally high across the analyzed cities.
* **Cleanliness** represents the most visible rating gap.
* Historical listing trends demonstrate the marketplace's sensitivity to **regulation and global travel disruptions**.
